Neomil Desktop Design

How the Neomil Hyprland theme should look before any of it is built. Every part is taken from the recreated reference screens: the header badges, the tab plate, the outlined dark plates, the solid selected row, the printed echo, and the measured line weights.

Use the switches to compare the two targets, the two bar heights and the two window styles. Actual size shows the scene at 3840 pixels wide, as on the display. Motion plays the proposed animations; Replay runs them again.

What stock Hyprland can and cannot draw

Can, by configuration
  • 2 px borders in the bright and dim inks
  • Cut corners on windows, all four alike: rounding with rounding_power 1.0, which terra already sets
  • Gaps, the wallpaper ground, stepped animations
  • Our own bar for both bar designs
  • Launcher, notifications and lock drawn by our own cp-eras-ui programs
Needs more than configuration
  • Red title plate on the focused window: a title-bar plugin or one of our own
  • The printed echo around a window: possibly a hard offset shadow, to be tried
  • Corners cut as the art cuts them, top-right and bottom-left only: stock Hyprland treats all four corners alike
Not decided here
  • Application interiors (terminal colours, browser, editor themes) are stand-ins
  • Bar content: which status cells, and what they are called
  • Durations of each animation
