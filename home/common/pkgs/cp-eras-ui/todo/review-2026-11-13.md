## 1. Ink on filled surfaces

The hypothesis this item started with -- one shared cause in the role or
colour of ink on a selected fill -- **was half right, and wrong about
where**. Measured against the originals (coverage: the share of pixels
darker than the fill/ink midpoint inside a text box) there are two
causes, and neither is the colour: the inks were already as dark as the
reference or darker.

- [x] **Trace weights too light** (commit `45aa5f3`). Neo-kitsch's
  ENTER / LOGIN (trace said weight 300 with 4.1 tracking; the reference
  is a wide face at normal spacing with stems of 0.077 em, where Rajdhani
  300 is 0.030 and 500 is 0.073 -- now 500, stretched 1.42, no tracking)
  and EMAIL panel body (now 500); Neomil's selected mail row and Switch
  Weapon (now 500; the Switch Weapon trace ink #59171b was lighter than
  any reference pixel and disagreed with the Rust's `on_select` #1a0405,
  so the trace took the Rust's value). Entropism's BRAINDANCE caption
  drew 600 against its trace's 700. Traces and Rust corrected together;
  four goldens refreshed, each changed only inside the element.
  Coverage reference / before / after: ENTER 13.4 / 0.2 / 13.5; EMAIL
  panel 15.4 / 8.2 / 11.1; Neomil row 16.7 / 8.7 / 12.0; Switch Weapon
  16.9 / 10.0 / 14.1; BRAINDANCE caption 14.5 / 8.1 / 12.9.
- [x] **Glyph edges blended in linear light: the glyph-atlas route.**
  Confirmed: re-blending a trace's own coverage in linear light lands its
  ink density on our render (Entropism mail row 11.2 vs 11.2, NEXT 17.1 vs
  16.9). wgpu blends coverage in linear light where the traces (rsvg) and
  the reference blend encoded values, so dark ink on a light fill renders
  thin with identical font, weight, size and colour. Fixed for text drawn
  through the glyph atlas by a coverage correction in the glyph fragment
  shader, in a vendored cryoglyph 0.1.0 (`vendor/cryoglyph/PATCHES.md`,
  `src/ink.rs`): for dark inks the coverage is raised to where a linear
  blend matches the encoded one against an assumed fill lightness of
  0.78, mixed in by a smooth window on the ink's luminance that is
  exactly zero at Y >= 0.085, where the shader takes the upstream
  expression. Light-on-dark text is bit-identical: every changed pixel in
  all 25 cases lies inside a dark-ink glyph quad. Median ours/trace
  coverage, before -> after: Entropism 0.85 -> 0.98, Kitsch 0.83 -> 1.02,
  Neo-kitsch 0.83 -> 0.99, Neomil 0.89 -> 1.07. 19 goldens refreshed.
  Known limits: Neomil overshoots mildly because its red fill is darker
  than the assumed 0.78 (Switch Weapon 16.9 trace, 19.1 ours); two inks
  just above the window (Kitsch #8a3f28, #6b4d08) keep the linear look on
  this route; a custom palette with ink below Y 0.085 on a dark ground
  would draw slightly heavier, and there is no runtime opt-out (the
  unused `Params._pad` uniform is the place for one). Measured on
  software Vulkan only.
- [ ] **The MSAA outline route is not fixed.** Text drawn as tessellated
  outlines (anything under `scale_nonuniform` / `.stretched()`, the
  scene's cached outline runs `ReusableWide` and `QuarterTurn Tracked`,
  rotated text) is resolved from an MSAA buffer holding ink and fill
  together, so no shader can tell which is the ink. Still thin:
  Entropism's selected mail row, message title bar, REPORT SPAM and
  BRAINDANCE caption; Neo-kitsch's ENTER / LOGIN and selected mailbox
  row; Kitsch's ENTER and EVENTS; Neomil's NEW pill. Plan, sized by a
  reverted experiment: stem darkening -- stroke each dark-ink outline
  after filling it, about 0.3 px (mail row 12.8 -> 14.1 against a trace
  of 14.6; 0.5 px overshoots), keyed on the ink's ROLE in the scene
  rather than its luminance, at `fill_text`'s non-uniform fallback
  (`vendor/iced_wgpu/src/geometry.rs`) and the two direct `fill(path,
  color)` sites in `src/screens/scene.rs`. It changes glyph shape
  slightly (counters), where the atlas fix changes only blending.
- Not this problem, found on the way (untracked until scheduled):
  Neomil's store CUSTOMER bar is light text on a darker bar in the
  reference (fill #872c2f, ink ~#f53535); ours uses `Dim`/`Fg` roles that
  the nix theme maps to #a32226 / #de2e2e, so the bar is too bright and
  the text too dim. Entropism's NEXT ink is fine; its fill is too minty
  (`SAGE_SOLID` #a6d3a7 against the trace's #8aac8c and the reference's
  #87ab89). The BRAINDANCE caption strip is missing its dark outer frame.
  Neomil's hover label in `components.svg` and `primary_states` still
  uses #59171b, probably the same trace error. Entropism's idle captions
  are 600 against the trace's 700 (light-on-dark, so out of scope here).
  The review's wording that Entropism's reference ink is "firm dark" is
  not borne out by samples: the reference ink is greener and lighter than
  ours; the difference is coverage.
