## 1. Ink on filled surfaces

The hypothesis this item started with -- one shared cause in the role or
colour of ink on a selected fill -- **was half right, and wrong about
where**. Measured against the originals (coverage: the share of pixels
darker than the fill/ink midpoint inside a text box) there are two
causes, and neither is the colour: the inks were already as dark as the
reference or darker.

- [x] **Trace weights too light** (commit `45aa5f3`). Neo-kitsch's
  ENTER / LOGIN (trace said weight 300 with 4.1 tracking; the reference
  is a wide face at normal spacing with stems of 0.077 em, where Rajdhani
  300 is 0.030 and 500 is 0.073 -- now 500, stretched 1.42, no tracking)
  and EMAIL panel body (now 500); Neomil's selected mail row and Switch
  Weapon (now 500; the Switch Weapon trace ink #59171b was lighter than
  any reference pixel and disagreed with the Rust's `on_select` #1a0405,
  so the trace took the Rust's value). Entropism's BRAINDANCE caption
  drew 600 against its trace's 700. Traces and Rust corrected together;
  four goldens refreshed, each changed only inside the element.
  Coverage reference / before / after: ENTER 13.4 / 0.2 / 13.5; EMAIL
  panel 15.4 / 8.2 / 11.1; Neomil row 16.7 / 8.7 / 12.0; Switch Weapon
  16.9 / 10.0 / 14.1; BRAINDANCE caption 14.5 / 8.1 / 12.9.
- [x] **Glyph edges blended in linear light: the glyph-atlas route.**
  Confirmed: re-blending a trace's own coverage in linear light lands its
  ink density on our render (Entropism mail row 11.2 vs 11.2, NEXT 17.1 vs
  16.9). wgpu blends coverage in linear light where the traces (rsvg) and
  the reference blend encoded values, so dark ink on a light fill renders
  thin with identical font, weight, size and colour. Fixed for text drawn
  through the glyph atlas by a coverage correction in the glyph fragment
  shader, in a vendored cryoglyph 0.1.0 (`vendor/cryoglyph/PATCHES.md`,
  `src/ink.rs`): for dark inks the coverage is raised to where a linear
  blend matches the encoded one against an assumed fill lightness of
  0.78, mixed in by a smooth window on the ink's luminance that is
  exactly zero at Y >= 0.085, where the shader takes the upstream
  expression. Light-on-dark text is bit-identical: every changed pixel in
  all 25 cases lies inside a dark-ink glyph quad. Median ours/trace
  coverage, before -> after: Entropism 0.85 -> 0.98, Kitsch 0.83 -> 1.02,
  Neo-kitsch 0.83 -> 0.99, Neomil 0.89 -> 1.07. 19 goldens refreshed.
  Known limits: Neomil overshoots mildly because its red fill is darker
  than the assumed 0.78 (Switch Weapon 16.9 trace, 19.1 ours); two inks
  just above the window (Kitsch #8a3f28, #6b4d08) keep the linear look on
  this route; a custom palette with ink below Y 0.085 on a dark ground
  would draw slightly heavier, and there is no runtime opt-out (the
  unused `Params._pad` uniform is the place for one). Measured on
  software Vulkan only.
- [x] **The MSAA outline route: stem darkening.** Text drawn as
  tessellated outlines is resolved from an MSAA buffer holding ink and
  fill together, so no shader can tell which is the ink. Fixed in
  `src/widgets/stem.rs`: a dark on-fill ink's outline is stroked after it
  is filled, by `GAIN x (midpoint_coverage(Y) - 0.5) x window(Y) x alpha`
  device px (0.15 px for black, 0.10 for #1f2a1c), sharing cryoglyph's
  `ASSUMED_FILL` and curve. Keyed on role AND luminance, because neither
  alone works: ground roles (`Fg`, `Dim`, `Mid`, `Border`, ...) never
  darken, which excludes Neomil's #a32226 (`Dim`, Y 0.091, darker than
  on-fill #7b5438 at 0.108); `OnSelect` and `Fixed` inks pass a smooth
  window that is full to Y 0.15 and zero from 0.20. Device px, not design
  px: the thinning lives in the one-device-pixel antialiased edge.
  **Corrections to what this item said before**: the sites were not just
  `fill_text`'s fallback and two in `scene.rs` -- `mail.rs` `label_keep`
  and `login.rs` `legend_text` carry the Entropism mail rows and the
  ENTER legends; "BRAINDANCE caption" means the selected hub tile's
  caption strip; the 7 px BETTERLIFE TEC lines on the stores are on this
  route too; and pure role keying is impossible, most on-fill inks being
  `Fixed` constants. The vendored fallback is untouched.
  GAIN 0.5 is deliberately conservative, fitted on ink mass over 27 runs
  (median ours/trace 0.92 -> 0.99 at 1600x900, 0.96 -> 0.97 at 4K; at
  GAIN 1.0 eleven runs overshoot by up to 20%). So at 1600x900 Entropism's
  selected row and title bar improve but stay under the trace (0.75 ->
  0.92, 0.78 -> 0.87, the FROM line 0.63 -> 0.77); at 4K they reach
  0.93-0.96. Four runs overshoot by 6-8% (Kitsch ENTER 1.08), each
  already near its trace before. In all 25 cases every changed pixel is
  dark ink on a fill and got darker; 16 cases are byte-identical;
  light-on-dark outline text has 0 px changed. 9 goldens refreshed. No
  new outline extraction; the cost is stroke tessellation per frame for
  darkened runs. The coverage-threshold metric used earlier in this
  section is not monotonic on thin text (it read 16.8 -> 8.5 on a line
  that visibly darkened); ink mass is the metric to use.
- [x] **Both ink fixes seen on the real GPU** (2026-11-13, terra, RTX
  4070, 3840x2160, installed package). The Neomil mailbox's selected row
  and Switch Weapon label are visibly firmer than in that morning's live
  capture, and the light text beside them is unchanged. Judged by eye
  from captures of one screen in one era; not measured, and the other
  three eras were not run live (Neomil is the installed theme).
- Limits left as they are: the fill is assumed at lightness 0.78, so
  Neomil's darker red is slightly over-corrected; at an output scale
  other than 1 the stroke width is in logical px; translucent runs stroke
  over their own fill (Kitsch store BETTERLIFE's echo stack); a `Fixed`
  or `OnSelect` ink below Y 0.20 on a dark ground in a custom palette
  would be thickened by up to 0.15 px; the tiny-skia backend, which
  already blends encoded values, would get the stroke without needing it;
  other traced text art (Neo-kitsch `LabelArt`) is not grown.
- Not this problem, found on the way (untracked until scheduled):
  Neomil's store CUSTOMER bar is light text on a darker bar in the
  reference (fill #872c2f, ink ~#f53535); ours uses `Dim`/`Fg` roles that
  the nix theme maps to #a32226 / #de2e2e, so the bar is too bright and
  the text too dim. Entropism's NEXT ink is fine; its fill is too minty
  (`SAGE_SOLID` #a6d3a7 against the trace's #8aac8c and the reference's
  #87ab89). The BRAINDANCE caption strip is missing its dark outer frame.
  Neomil's hover label in `components.svg` and `primary_states` still
  uses #59171b, probably the same trace error. Entropism's idle captions
  are 600 against the trace's 700 (light-on-dark, so out of scope here).
  The review's wording that Entropism's reference ink is "firm dark" is
  not borne out by samples: the reference ink is greener and lighter than
  ours; the difference is coverage.

## 2. Kitsch label typeface

- [x] **Section labels in Michroma** (SIL OFL 1.1, from nixpkgs
  `google-fonts`, staged into `fonts/` by `default.nix` and `shell.nix`
  like the other faces; `OFL.txt` installed with the package). USER,
  DESCRIPTION, SECURITY LEVEL, DEVICE SOFTWARE and the notes beside the
  lettered badges. The traces themselves named the wrong face (Rajdhani
  stretched x1.3 / x1.37), and the Rust had drifted from them on the
  mailbox and store (no stretch). Reference label: cap 7.9, stem
  1.1-1.33, USER 38.3 wide; before 25-34 wide with stem 1.9; now 39 wide,
  stem 1.22-1.45, baselines within 1 px. Chosen over Saira Expanded,
  Krona One, Syncopate, Orbitron, Aldrich and seven others by width and
  letterform; only the four Kitsch cases change, the other 21 are
  byte-identical. Shared files (`style.rs`, `scene.rs`, `bar.rs`,
  `screens/mail.rs`, `screens/login.rs`) gained a face and two additive
  options whose defaults reproduce the old behaviour.
- [x] **Weight inversions, each confirmed by measurement first.** Mailbox
  subjects Regular 18 -> Medium 20 (stem 1.35 -> 1.83, reference 1.70);
  "from:" lines Bold -> Medium (2.03-2.33 -> 1.58-1.60, reference
  1.41-1.47); store nav labels follow their trace again, Rajdhani 500
  24.5 (the Rust had drawn Regular 22); GUEST 7702 Medium 20 -> SemiBold
  22 in bright mint. The store's lower list was a SIZE error, not weight:
  the reference is 1.25x larger, now Medium 19.5.
- [x] **Badge notes in Krona One** (Matt, 2026-11-14; SIL OFL 1.1, from
  the same pinned `google-fonts`, wired exactly as Michroma). Michroma has
  one weight and came out thin and dim at a 4.2 px cap, where the
  reference sets these lines bold. Fitted on all 32 photographed lines:
  size 5.46, NO stretch, tracking 0.64, word space 0.45. Krona One's
  letters already match the reference's letter widths; the missing width
  was all in the spacing (reference letter gaps median 1.25, word gaps
  4.2), so stretching, which the comparison sheet had assumed, would have
  matched extents with letters 10% too wide. Line-end error now within
  2.8 px (Michroma: up to 6.6). No colour role change: the reference
  notes are as bright as the badge box beside them, which is the role
  they already had. Section labels stay Michroma and did not move: every
  changed pixel is inside a notes box, 21 cases byte-identical.
  Shared code gained two additive things, defaults unchanged: mail runs
  can be tracked (`Run.tracking`, a glyph-by-glyph branch in
  `mail.rs`), and Krona One runs snap to whole pixel rows in `scene.rs`,
  `mail.rs` and `login.rs`, without which the notes sat a pixel high at
  1x. Traces place each word at an absolute `<tspan x>`: librsvg 2.62
  drops letter-spacing at `tspan dx` boundaries.
  Left as it is: Krona One's bowls (O, C, G, S, 0) are rounder than the
  reference's square ones, visible side by side; at 1600x900 a 4-row cap
  quantises (S reads near 5) -- clean at 4K; the 1x trace render is about
  4 px short per line (pango quantises tracked positions), so G1/G2 carry
  that offset.
- Approximate, left: "+2 Modules Slots"
  and W); "+2 Modules Slots" runs 8 px long; nav caps 1.4 px tall; "from:"
  lines about 0.15 px heavy.
- Found in Kitsch, not this item: the boxed badge letters A/B/C/D are

## 4. Texture and veneer

- [ ] **Print overlay: prototype built, off by default, awaiting Matt's
  verdict.** `src/print.rs`, a post-process pass in the vendored
  iced_wgpu (`vendor/iced_wgpu/PATCHES.md`, second change), measurements
  in `docs/print-overlay.md`. `print::DEFAULT_STRENGTH` is 0, so every
  golden is unchanged; `CP_ERAS_UI_PRINT=1` wears the fit.
  **What the study found, which changes the brief**: the references are
  lossless digital exports, not photographs (the Neomil dashboard's red
  tiles are exactly #ef3333 with zero variance), so the "printed" look is
  designed in -- and it is NOT scanlines and grain over the whole screen.
  Clear ground and the large fills carry no scanlines at all (row
  amplitude 0.01 levels); a global scanline multiply would contradict the
  reference on its largest areas. What both eras have everywhere is
  faint, soft, scanlined COPIES of every bright element, offset outward
  from the screen centre in proportion to distance (a uniform scale of
  about 0.5-0.8% about the centre; Neomil shows a second copy at twice
  that). That is what the pass draws: two scaled, softened copies of the
  frame composited as lighten in encoded sRGB, gated off inside light
  fills so dark ink stays firm, then per-channel grain (Entropism's sage
  fills: std 4.5 in the reference, 0 before, 4.8 after).
  Limits: no bloom (needs two more passes), blue grain under-fit, the few
  Neomil fills that do carry scanlines (active login card, store VIDEO)
  are not reproduced. Cost on software Vulkan: +32% a frame at 1600x900,
  +68% at 4K; real-GPU cost unmeasured.
- [ ] **If the overlay is accepted, remove the per-element echo
  drawing**, or Neomil's dashboard, store and login are doubled (the pass
  adds copies where elements already draw their own: GO HOME body and
  heading, tile labels, header printing, margin codes, brands, store card
  titles, login header echoes). Then close the radial echo and
  "background material" items across `todo/` as superseded. Still wanted
  afterwards: per-element material fields (panel and badge scanlines,
  active card and VIDEO scan modulation), non-radial designed duplicates,
  bloom if Entropism's glow matters.
- [ ] **Veneer, new direction (Matt, 2026-11-14): recover the splines
  and how they were replicated.** He looked at both generated passes
  against the reference (second pass `fa177ae` on the branch: lines stay
  lines and pinch to a core, irregular grain, clean at 1600) and it still
  does not look right. His read, which the evidence fits: the reference
  grain is constructed vector art -- a few key curves replicated by a
  blend, offset or mirror -- not wood, so a noise model cannot match it
  and recovering the construction can. In progress on the branch: trace
  every line at 4K, test blend / offset / translate / mirror / warp
  hypotheses with residuals, fit the key curves, rebuild each surface
  from the construction alone. Whether the result ships as a texture or
  as real vector paths is open again (decision 3 chose a texture when the
  alternative was hand-fitted stripes).
- [ ] **Neo-kitsch veneer as a generated texture: built, on branch
  `veneer-texture` (`3f589fa`), awaiting Matt's verdict.** Not on main:
  the look is not clearly right and it changes shared rendering (a
  layered canvas so marks drawn over a veneer image stay above it).
  `scripts/make-veneer.py` is a deterministic procedural generator
  (numpy only, reads no image, so no reference pixels are in the
  repository; a passthru test regenerates the six PNGs and compares them
  byte for byte), 942 KB of assets at 2.4 texels per design px so 4K
  samples them 1:1. Six surfaces: dashboard EMAIL card and panel, mailbox
  row, store card, SMG button, BASKET band. The login ENTER / LOGIN bars
  are flat in the reference and stay flat. 22 of 25 cases byte-identical;
  one stray pixel outside the veneered boxes on the store (a hairline
  raised into the band's layer). Orchestrator's read of the evidence:
  better than stripes -- figure, a panel fold, a card streak, clean lines
  at 4K -- but the store streak merges into a soft dark band where the
  reference keeps resolvable converging lines, the figures are more
  regular than the reference's, and the panel is muddy at 1600x900. The
  widget mail app still uses its old synthesised veneer. Goldens not
  refreshed on the branch.
