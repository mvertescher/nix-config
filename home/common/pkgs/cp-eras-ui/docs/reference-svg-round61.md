## Kitsch certification lettering

The intended Rajdhani Bold face, size 3.2, tracking .45 and accepted
native alpha .66531 remain correctly selected. SVG alpha .35 and the
independent lower-rule calibration are intentional renderer fits. Their
difference is not itself a font defect. Fresh packaged captures at
3840×2160, 1600×900 and 1537×947 verify the current baseline; the 1600
frame exactly matches its golden.

The photograph does not establish a shared missing letter contour. In an
84-pixel letter core, source green deficits of at least 20 occupy
31/21/23/29 pixels across the four cards; only two pixels belong to all
four sets. At deficit 30 their intersection is empty. Local photographic
coverage and raster phase remain confounders; even the `RG5` reading is
unverified.

One frozen SVG trial moves the upper print one 4K pixel left and up.
Letter-core RGB MAE changes are:

| Viewport | Card 1 | Selected card 2 | Card 3 | Clipped card 4 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 3840×2160 | +2.07 | −2.35 | −1.23 | +2.20 |
| 1600×900 | +4.50 | −5.22 | −2.28 | +4.39 |
| 1537×947 | +1.92 | −2.67 | +0.64 | +2.50 |

Negative is better. Card 1 and card 4 lose at every size, including their
wider upper-print crops; card 3 reverses at fractional size. The lower
rule stays exact. This translation is rejected without a native port,
font sweep or arbitrary glyph trace.

The initial width-only fractional SVG render was invalid for full-frame
comparison: 66,125 RGB pixels differ from an explicit top-left 1537×947
viewport. Both baseline and trial are rerendered correctly. Their fixed
RG5 scores happen to remain unchanged, and the corrected baseline is
byte-identical to Astra's independent render. Invalid historical rasters
are retained separately in scratch rather than used as evidence.

## Neomil maker-text scan modulation

These maker labels use accepted geometric paths; no font substitution is
involved. Source-to-native row-gain profiles correlate strongly within
PRECISION/LIQUID and separately within POLYMER/MUSCLE: respectively
.985/.789/.959 and .967/.950/.977 at 4K/1600/fractional size. Common stroke
geometry and a modeled ground can contribute; this does not identify a
physical waveform or renderer recipe.

One cosine multiplier uses the adjacent printing's frozen 1.984934 design
pixel period. Only 4K PRECISION primary support trains its coefficients
(-.014022, .041987, .004375); those coefficients then remain fixed across
words and sizes. This is an image-domain counterfactual against a no-text
SVG proxy ground, not a rendered SVG/native implementation or a recovered
photographic background.

LIQUID's 4K primary support worsens 15.351→15.404 despite its aggregate

rescue is unsupported. Further work needs an independently established
waveform and support before another actual rendering trial.

Astra reproduces six source projections, six Kitsch baseline/trial SVG

## Entropism ordinary rifle seam

The source provides a concrete repeated structural seam in ordinary
cards 2 and 3, at design origins (783,260) and (1105,260). Its upper
return, diagonal and lower join are missing from the existing two-tone
masks. The selected rifle has a separately measured inverse drawing and
stays unchanged; card 4 retains its persistent clip.

A width .8 centerline follows the visible seam from (205.125,136.25)
through (206.791667,133.958333), (207.4,134.2) and (219.5,147.5), then
curves through control (222,149.2) to (224.2,149.6) and ends at
(225,149.6). A filled polygon intersects that stroke with the accepted
bright contour offline. This removes .1283 design square pixels that
would otherwise escape the upper silhouette. The resulting 78 vertices
add one ordinary-only vector shape with ink #a5cca4; no image, filter or
runtime clipping mechanism is introduced.

The polygon expansion itself is not a pixel-exact replay of the stroked
trial: it changes 84/50/35 pixels at 4K/1600/fractional size by at most two
channel levels. The final clipped shape is compared directly with the
current baseline and photograph, rather than assigning those differences
to the clipping alone. Whole-stock RGB L1 changes are:

| Viewport | Card 2 | Card 3 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 3840×2160 | 76450→74088 | 83852→81355 |
| 1600×900 | 12408→11790 | 14872→14245 |
| 1537×947 | 13242→12678 | 13162→12671 |

Straight and lower-join regions improve in every case. The fractional
card-3 upper join loses 44 RGB L1 units; that local loss remains explicit.
Left-exterior and right-cutout controls are exact. All 390/108/99 changed
pixels lie within the two ordinary stock boxes, with exact alpha and all
other artwork unchanged. The separate lower segmented band visible in
the photograph is still missing; this correction does not complete E4.

The accepted shape is integrated in the source trace and copied into the
component sheet. Three production SVG renders match the reviewed candidate
exactly. The complete ordinary-card component matches its parent XML after
insignificant whitespace normalization; full/half sheet differences remain
confined to its stock seam.

The native trial is **rejected**. A fresh current-source library first
reproduces packaged Entropism at all three sizes and packaged Kitsch at
4K exactly. One provisional semantic Mid path at constant opacity .065
then changes 302/62/61 pixels by only one channel level. Its whole-stock
RGB L1 changes are +5/+7, +18/+24 and +13/+26 for cards 2/3 at the three
sizes: every whole-stock comparison loses. The native bright stock is
already darker than the SVG, so matching this seam needs a coordinated
review of the local native ink, not a blind transfer or another isolated
alpha sweep. No Rust or golden change is accepted, and no hover/held,
opening, custom-palette or timing claim is made for that rejected trial.
