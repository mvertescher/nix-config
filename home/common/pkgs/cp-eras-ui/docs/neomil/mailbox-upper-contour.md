# Mailbox upper contour and cartridge terminal

The source is `images/img-08-main.png` at 3840×2160; trace coordinates are on the 1600×900 design grid. The lower reader corner and inward side step had already been corrected. This pass changes only the upper panel edge and cartridge terminal ribs.

Native red-channel scans locate the panel's main left stroke at x729.17. Its top center is y313.33 from x740 through x1040, eases to y312.5 by x1240 and stays there through x1439.17. The upper-right chamfer joins the vertical x1450 edge at y323.33. The earlier trace used a level y312 top ending at x1442 and joined x1450 at y320. The new contour is shared by the material mask and bright outline; the separate thick vertical stem begins at y323.33. The lower inward step at (1440.5548,554.0285), square bottom y699.3177, side bar, opening clip and text remain unchanged.

At native resolution in the clean corner x1390..1460/y306..338, bright-edge IoU against the source (red−green >85) rises from .359 to .835 after the contour and stem move. False bright pixels there fall from 26 to 4 when the stem is moved. Over the entire x720..1460/y306..338 upper strip, IoU rises from .121 to .768. These checks isolate the high-contrast edge; the source's dim top/right echo lines and x1100..1400 brightness modulation remain separate material and printing work. Scratch source/SVG crops and fit renders are `/tmp/cp-eras-completion/mailbox-panel-{origin,corner}-grid.png` and `/tmp/cp-eras-completion/mailbox-panel-fit-stem-native.png`.

The source cartridge cap has approximately three native pixels, or 1.25 design units, between dark ribs, measured by the red-channel autocorrelation along its sloped terminal. The old 17 strokes used 1.6667-unit pitch. Both normal and selected terminals now use 22 open strokes at 1.25 pitch. The selected source strip has red p10/p50/p90 of about 220/229/251 with green around 48–53; its former #59171b stroke was too dark. The selected stroke is #dc3035 at one design unit. In a held-out native strip band, selected red MAE falls 61.77→20.38 and RMSE 79.37→28.42. The normal terminal retains its prior dark-red tone because the same local color change did not improve its measured source fit; its material and fine echoes remain unresolved. The source/old/candidate terminal review is `/tmp/cp-eras-completion/mailbox-terminal-fit1-review.png`.
